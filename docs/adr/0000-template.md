# Architecture Decision Record template

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: names or agent identities
- Work packages: MM-XXX

## Context

What problem must be solved? Which V2 invariants constrain the solution?

## Decision

State the decision in one paragraph.

## Evidence

Measured facts, tests, or constraints that justify the decision. Service extraction
requires independently measured scaling, isolation, availability, or team-boundary
evidence; taste is insufficient.

## Consequences

Positive and negative consequences, including invalidation impact.

## Alternatives considered

List alternatives and why they were rejected.
